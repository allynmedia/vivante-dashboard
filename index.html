Section 1
Coverage Summary

Vivante Turtle Creek generated 11 earned media mentions between March 1 and May 12, 2026, reaching an estimated potential audience of 9.7 million — a 15.7% increase over the prior period. While mention volume declined 21.4%, the reach increase signals that coverage is landing in higher-authority outlets and on higher-reach social accounts than previously.

The dominant story in circulation is the Turtle Creek development narrative — specifically, the project's proximity to the Clover Tower construction site and the evolution of the Vivante brand in the Dallas market. This conversation is being driven primarily by a highly engaged Reddit community (r/Dallasdevelopment) and picked up by Dallas-area commercial real estate press, most notably ConnectCRE.

A notable caveat: several mentions originating in France and Canada reference "Bretagne Vivante," a French environmental organization, and should be excluded from net brand analysis. When filtered for client-relevant mentions, the effective count is closer to 7 mentions, all U.S.-based, concentrated in Texas and focused on real estate development.

Coverage sentiment is overwhelmingly neutral (57%), which is common for a pre-opening development project. The absence of positive sentiment reflects a missed opportunity: there is no proactive PR program currently generating coverage that frames Vivante on its own terms — as a luxury senior living destination, not merely as a construction site near other projects.

Section 2
Narrative & Sentiment Analysis

The Vivante brand currently exists in public conversation almost entirely as a supporting character in someone else's story. In every major mention this period, Vivante Turtle Creek is referenced as a geographic marker — "just north of Vivante Turtle Creek" — rather than as the subject of coverage. This is a meaningful reputational signal: the brand has not yet established an independent narrative in the Dallas market.

"Vivante is being talked about, but not on its own terms. The brand exists as a landmark in other people's stories — not as the subject of its own."

The dominant themes in coverage — development, construction, cost, area, turtle creek — confirm the conversation is entirely developer-driven. Words like "living," "human," and "vision" appear in the theme cloud but are buried beneath real estate terminology, suggesting that Vivante's lifestyle positioning has not yet broken through into earned media.

The single most significant narrative risk identified this period is the project downsizing story. Multiple mentions reference the reduction from a proposed 30-story tower to a 15-story building. This story is currently framed neutrally — as a construction fact — but it has potential to shift negative if picked up as a scaled-back or compromised project. Three anger-coded mentions clustered around April 26 align precisely with this coverage and represent the first credible reputational signal of the monitoring period.

On the positive side, the lifestyle content Vivante is producing on social — dining experiences, wellness, construction progress — is generating its strongest engagement when it is authentic and forward-looking. This content strategy is the foundation of a proactive narrative, but it has not yet been leveraged into earned media placements.

Section 3
Competitive Media Position

Viewed against direct Dallas-area competitors, Vivante's earned media position is the most underdeveloped of the set — with one significant exception. Despite generating only 16 press articles over the past 13 months (versus CC Young's 177 and Atria's 286), Vivante's Unique Visitor Monthly reach of 369 million is the highest of the three brands monitored. Vivante's coverage, when it lands, reaches more people than its competitors' coverage. The problem is frequency, not quality of placement.

Section 4
Recommended PR & Image Tactics

The following recommendations are prioritized by urgency and grounded in data from this reporting period. Each is tied directly to Vivante's pre-opening brand-building objectives.

01
Urgent — Act This Month
Get Ahead of the Downsizing Narrative Before Construction Begins
Three anger-coded mentions clustered around April 26 align with coverage of the project's reduction from 30 to 15 stories. This story is currently neutral in tone but alive in the Dallas development community and will resurface at groundbreaking. Allyn recommends preparing a proactive narrative document that frames the 15-story design as a deliberate architectural decision — not a scaled-back compromise — with a quote from the developer or project lead. Distribute to Dallas Morning News real estate desk, Dallas Business Journal and ConnectCRE before construction commences. Waiting until the story is written without your input is a preventable risk.
02
Urgent — Act This Month
Fix the Turtle Creek Landing Page Before Spending Another Dollar on Ads
The /turtle-creek-new page receives 499 monthly sessions — the primary destination for all Turtle Creek awareness spending — but converts at just 0.20%, the lowest key event rate on the site. The homepage converts at 3.34%. Users are spending nearly a minute on the page, meaning they are interested — but leaving without taking action. This page needs a prominent VIP interest form above the fold, a clear call to action, and at least one social proof element. Every dollar spent driving traffic here is underperforming by a factor of 16× relative to the homepage. This is the highest-ROI fix available to the marketing budget right now.
03
Urgent — Act This Month
Reallocate Paid Search Budget Away from the Memory Care Campaign
The Memory Care campaign spent $3,302 and generated just 5 conversions at $660 per conversion — 16× less efficient than the Brand campaign. The keyword "memory care assisted living" alone spent $1,065 with zero conversions. The OC Assisted Living Terms campaign converts at $364, nearly 9× the Brand campaign rate. Pause the Memory Care campaign immediately, reduce OC Assisted Living by $5,000–8,000/month, and reinvest in the YouTube Demand Gen campaign which converts at $55 on 65 conversions — the most efficient non-brand source in the account and significantly underweighted relative to its performance.
04
Near-Term — Next 30 Days
Pitch a Feature Story on Vivante's Design Vision to Dallas Morning News
Vivante's UVM of 369 million — the highest of any brand in the competitive set — means a single well-placed story generates more audience exposure than a full month of CC Young's press activity. Allyn recommends developing a pitch around the "Living Material" design philosophy and the Turtle Creek property's relationship to its natural surroundings, targeting the Dallas Morning News real estate and lifestyle desks and D Magazine's home and design coverage. The pitch should lead with what makes Vivante architecturally and experientially distinct from every other senior living project in Dallas — not with project specs. The groundbreaking is a natural hook for timing.
05
Near-Term — Next 30 Days
Engage r/Dallasdevelopment Directly and Establish a Brand Presence
The Reddit community r/Dallasdevelopment generated 33 of the 35 total earned media engagements this period — by far the most engaged audience in the monitoring data. The top author reached 1 million users with two posts. This community is actively following the Turtle Creek project. Allyn recommends creating an official Vivante presence in this community and beginning with transparent, informational participation — answering questions, sharing construction updates, providing context on design decisions. This is a community relations play that costs nothing and carries significant narrative control value at a moment when the project downsizing story is still forming.
06
Near-Term — Next 30 Days
Double Down on Construction Content and Increase Instagram Frequency
The three highest-performing posts this period were Instagram posts — construction progress (19 likes), dining (15 likes), wellness (13 likes). The three lowest were Facebook posts with 1 like each. The data is unambiguous: authentic, progress-oriented Instagram content is what this audience responds to. Increase posting frequency to 5× per week on Instagram, front-loading construction milestones and team content. Every post should include a direct link to the Turtle Creek signup page — link clicks are currently at zero, which means the audience is not being moved from social to the conversion funnel.
07
Ongoing Priority
Build Organic Search Authority for Non-Brand Senior Living Terms
Vivante holds #1 for every branded keyword but ranks at #26 for "assisted living orange county" (880 monthly searches) and has no ranking for "assisted living huntington beach ca" — yet both are being bid on at $15–18 CPC. The site is paying significant monthly budget to compensate for an organic gap that content and link acquisition could close. Dedicated location landing pages for each service market, combined with outreach to A Place for Mom, Seniorly and Caring.com — all of which appear in the competitive keyword data — would reduce this paid dependency within 3–6 months. Referral traffic currently converts at 74.1% engagement rate, the highest of any channel, which directly supports the case for prioritizing these directory relationships.
Section 5
Upcoming Risks & Opportunities

The following factors are likely to generate media attention or require proactive positioning in the next 30–60 days, based on current coverage trajectories and the Dallas development calendar.

Risks to Monitor
Groundbreaking coverage: Construction commencement will trigger a new press cycle. Without proactive outreach, framing will be left to reporters working from public records — which currently emphasize the project downsizing.
Clover Tower narrative bleed: The adjacent project is generating its own press. Multiple Vivante mentions arise only because Vivante is cited as a nearby landmark. If Clover Tower coverage turns negative, Vivante risks being pulled into an unfavorable story by proximity.
Competitor content acceleration: CC Young published 18 social posts this period and generated top engagement scores of 31, 30 and 24 reactions. If Vivante does not accelerate output, the follower gap will widen further.
Mobile site performance: A 64 performance score with 950ms blocking time means paid ad traffic is arriving at a slow page. Any Google Ads quality score review could increase CPCs across all campaigns.
Opportunities to Pursue
Groundbreaking as a PR moment: A well-produced groundbreaking event with media invitations, renderings and a design spokesperson is a natural feature story for Dallas Morning News and D Magazine — the same event that carries risk also carries significant earned media opportunity.
Owning the luxury senior living design angle: No competitor is currently owning the architectural and design differentiation angle in Dallas press. Vivante's "Living Material" concept is a genuinely differentiated story with no competition for coverage.
VIP interest list as a media hook: Building a documented waitlist before opening is itself a story. "Dallas's most anticipated senior living community" is a legitimate pitch to Dallas Culturemap, D Magazine and Advocate Magazine.
Referral traffic quality: Referral sessions convert at 74.1% engagement and 5m 36s average session time — the best traffic quality on the site. Identifying and cultivating these specific referral sources should be an immediate priority.
