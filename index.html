Section 1
Flight Snapshot — October 6–November 17, 2026

Meta advertising for the 2026 Dallas YMCA Turkey Trot began on October 6, 2026 and runs through race morning on Thanksgiving Day, November 26. Every ad sends people to the official registration page on Race Roster, and Meta optimizes delivery for landing page views — people who click through and wait for that page to load.

Through November 17, the ads drove 28,694 landing page views from 724,694 impressions at a blended cost of $0.25 per view.

Section 3
What to Watch Through Race Day
01
New video ads. The Costume Video and Mascot Video ads target a broader family audience that includes parents of children from toddlers to teens. They also give people who have already seen the original ads something new, which helps prevent creative fatigue as the campaign builds toward race day. Since launch they have driven 9,008 landing page views combined, with the Costume Video at $0.26 per view and the Mascot Video at $0.62.
02
Start Line vs. Giant Turkey videos. The Start Line Video continues to deliver at $0.16 per landing page view, and Meta is directing nearly all of its ad group’s spend there. The Giant Turkey Video ($0.25 per view) is receiving only a small share.
03
Pacing check. Pacing was reviewed the week of November 9 and remains on track heading into the final push of Thanksgiving week.
04
Registrations. Landing page views show how many people reached the registration page, not how many signed up. Race Roster registration totals for the same period would show how many visits became registrations.
