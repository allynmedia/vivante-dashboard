Section 1
Coverage Summary

Vivante Turtle Creek generated 11 earned media mentions between March 1 and May 12, 2026, reaching an estimated potential audience of 9.7 million — a 15.7% increase over the prior period. While mention volume declined 21.4%, the reach increase signals that coverage is landing in higher-authority outlets and on higher-reach social accounts than previously.

The dominant story in circulation is the Turtle Creek development narrative — specifically, the project's proximity to the Clover Tower construction site and the evolution of the Vivante brand in the Dallas market. This conversation is being driven primarily by a highly engaged Reddit community (r/Dallasdevelopment) and picked up by Dallas-area commercial real estate press, most notably ConnectCRE.

A notable caveat: several mentions originating in France and Canada reference "Bretagne Vivante," a French environmental organization, and should be excluded from net brand analysis. When filtered for client-relevant mentions, the effective count is closer to 7 mentions, all U.S.-based, concentrated in Texas and focused on real estate development.

Coverage sentiment is overwhelmingly neutral (57%), which is common for a pre-opening development project. The absence of positive sentiment reflects a missed opportunity: there is no proactive PR program currently generating coverage that frames Vivante on its own terms — as a luxury senior living destination, not merely as a construction site near other projects.

Section 2
Narrative & Sentiment Analysis

The Vivante brand currently exists in public conversation almost entirely as a supporting character in someone else's story. In every major mention this period, Vivante Turtle Creek is referenced as a geographic marker — "just north of Vivante Turtle Creek" — rather than as the subject of coverage. This is a meaningful reputational signal: the brand has not yet established an independent narrative in the Dallas market.

"Vivante is being talked about, but not on its own terms. The brand exists as a landmark in other people's stories — not as the subject of its own."

The dominant themes in coverage — development, construction, cost, area, turtle creek — confirm the conversation is entirely developer-driven. Words like "living," "human," and "vision" appear in the theme cloud but are buried beneath real estate terminology, suggesting that Vivante's lifestyle positioning has not yet broken through into earned media.

The single most significant narrative risk identified this period is the project downsizing story. Multiple mentions reference the reduction from a proposed 30-story tower to a 15-story building. This story is currently framed neutrally — as a construction fact — but it has potential to shift negative if picked up as a scaled-back or compromised project. Three anger-coded mentions clustered around April 26 align precisely with this coverage and represent the first credible reputational signal of the monitoring period.

On the positive side, the lifestyle content Vivante is producing on social — dining experiences, wellness, construction progress — is generating its strongest engagement when it is authentic and forward-looking. This content strategy is the foundation of a proactive narrative, but it has not yet been leveraged into earned media placements.

Section 3
Competitive Media Position

Viewed against direct Dallas-area competitors, Vivante's earned media position is the most underdeveloped of the set — with one significant exception. Despite generating only 16 press articles over the past 13 months (versus CC Young's 177 and Atria's 286), Vivante's Unique Visitor Monthly reach of 369 million is the highest of the three brands monitored. Vivante's coverage, when it lands, reaches more people than its competitors' coverage. The problem is frequency, not quality of placement.
