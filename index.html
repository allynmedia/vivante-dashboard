Allyn Analyst Briefing — May 2026 · Vivante Turtle Creek
01The Turtle Creek Page Is Your Most Urgent Fix
The /turtle-creek-new landing page — the primary pre-sales destination for the product being built — receives 499 sessions per month but converts at just 0.20%, the lowest key event rate of any page on the site. The homepage converts at 3.34% by comparison. Visitors spending nearly a minute on that page are engaged but not taking action. This page needs a direct call to action, a newsletter signup or VIP interest form above the fold, and social proof content. Every dollar being spent building Turtle Creek awareness is landing on a page that fails to capture interest.
02$22,768 Is Buying Leads the Brand Campaign Earns for $41
The OC Assisted Living Terms campaign converts at $364 per conversion — nearly 9× the Brand campaign's $41.63 rate. The Memory Care campaign converts at $660 on just 5 conversions. The keyword "memory care assisted living" spent $1,065 with zero conversions. YouTube Demand Gen, meanwhile, converts at $55 on 65 conversions and is largely overlooked. Reduce OC Assisted Living budget by $5,000–8,000/month, pause Memory Care pending restructure, and reinvest in YouTube Demand Gen.
03Paid Social Is Clicking but Not Engaging
Paid Social drives 1,758 sessions but averages just 7 seconds per session and a 33.2% engagement rate. Cross-network traffic is worse: 14 seconds average on 3,661 sessions. Organic Social, by contrast, has a 60.4% engagement rate — meaning people who find Vivante naturally are far more interested than those driven by paid campaigns. There is a clear mismatch between ad creative and landing page experience. Review targeting and landing page alignment for all Meta campaigns.
04SEO Is Dominant on Brand, Invisible on Generics
Vivante holds #1 for every branded keyword. But "assisted living orange county" (880 monthly searches) ranks at #26, and "assisted living huntington beach ca" has no ranking at all — yet both are being bid on at $15–18 CPC. The site is paying to rank for terms it should own organically. Dedicated location landing pages and directory link acquisition from A Place for Mom, Seniorly and Caring.com would reduce this dependency within 3–6 months. The net loss of 2,036 backlinks this period compounds the problem over time.
05Mobile Performance Is Sending Paid Traffic to a Slow Page
Mobile Performance score is 64 with 950ms Total Blocking Time and 3.8s LCP in lab conditions. There are 667 KiB of unused JavaScript and 17 long main-thread tasks. This is a material issue when $35,500/month in paid traffic is arriving largely on mobile. A developer spending 2–3 days on JS cleanup, image optimization and caching could meaningfully improve both Quality Score in Google Ads and on-page conversion rates.
06Earned Media Is Underleveraged Given Vivante's UVM Position
Only 16 press articles in 13 months against CC Young's 177 and Atria's 286 — yet Vivante's UVM of 369M is the highest of the three. Coverage, when it lands, reaches more people than competitors' coverage. The project downsizing story is already in circulation and driving the only anger-coded social signals. Proactive press outreach around the updated 15-story design and construction commencement should begin now, not after ground is broken. The r/Dallasdevelopment community generated 33 of 35 total listening engagements and is an underutilized direct-engagement channel.
